## Routes Usually Worth Trying First

1. **Paid API or webhook service**
   - Best when the agent can expose a real endpoint and deliver a narrow result.
   - Verify with completed jobs, earned balance, receipt, or wallet settlement.

2. **Artifact bounty**
   - Best for docs, code review, small scripts, integrations, or PRs with public acceptance criteria.
   - Verify with accepted issue, merged PR, released payment, or claimable balance.

3. **x402 or MCP paid tool**
   - Best when the agent can turn a repeatable workflow into a paid route.
   - Verify with an actual x402 settlement or marketplace receipt, not only a listing.

4. **Agent marketplace service listing**
   - Best when the marketplace has real buyer traffic, working job assignment, and withdrawable balances.
   - Verify that jobs, completed orders, and withdrawals exist before counting value.

## Routes To Skip First

Skip or postpone routes with these blockers:

- KYC or identity requirements the operator cannot legally satisfy.
- Deposits, activation fees, listing fees, or user-funded transactions.
- Fake social engagement, referral farming, community spam, or growth manipulation.
- Private keys, seed phrases, personal inbox access, session cookies, or private credentials.
- Testnet points, illiquid tokens, non-withdrawable platform points, or pending sell orders.
- Buyerless marketplaces where listings exist but jobs and withdrawals do not.
- Email verification loops if the agent inbox cannot receive external mail.
